## Phase 1: Set Goal (PGP Goal-Setting System)

### US-001: Coach-Initiated PGP Goal Setting

**As a** coach (Sarah Martinez)  
**I want** to collaborate with teachers to set meaningful, specific professional growth goals  
**So that** our coaching sessions are focused and aligned with their development needs

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Coach can access teacher profile and initiate PGP goal setting process
- [ ] System displays teacher's subject area, grade level, and experience for context
- [ ] Coach can browse Louisiana Educator Rubric domains and indicators
- [ ] AI generates SMART goal suggestions based on teacher context and selected indicators
- [ ] Coach and teacher can collaboratively edit and refine the goal
- [ ] System saves final goal with progress tracking framework
- [ ] Goal appears in teacher dashboard and coach analytics

**Priority:** P0 - Critical  
**Success Metrics:** 90% of teachers have PGP goals set within 7 days of coach assignment  
**Business Impact:** Foundation for targeted feedback and measurable growth tracking

---

### US-002: AI-Assisted Goal Generation

**As a** coach (Sarah Martinez)  
**I want** AI to generate contextualized PGP goal suggestions  
**So that** I can focus on collaborative refinement rather than starting from scratch

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] System aggregates teacher context (subject, grade, experience, previous goals)
- [ ] AI generates 3-5 SMART goal options with rationale
- [ ] Goals align with selected Louisiana Educator Rubric indicators
- [ ] Coach can preview, edit, or regenerate suggestions
- [ ] Generated goals include specific, measurable outcomes
- [ ] System tracks goal generation usage for plan limits

**Priority:** P0 - Critical  
**Success Metrics:** 80% of coaches use AI suggestions as starting point for goal setting  
**Business Impact:** Reduces coach time investment and improves goal quality

---

### US-003: Teacher Goal Ownership and Progress Tracking

**As a** teacher (Michael Thompson)  
**I want** to see my professional growth goal and track my progress  
**So that** I can take ownership of my development and see clear advancement

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] Teacher dashboard prominently displays current PGP goal
- [ ] Progress slider shows goal completion percentage (0-100%)
- [ ] Teacher can update progress with evidence and reflection
- [ ] System connects goal progress to walkthrough feedback
- [ ] Achievement milestones trigger celebration notifications
- [ ] Goal appears in reflection prompts for context

**Priority:** P1 - Important  
**Success Metrics:** 85% of teachers actively update goal progress monthly  
**Business Impact:** Increases teacher engagement and ownership of growth journey
